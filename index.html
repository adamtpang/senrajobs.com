About

Learn from the inside. Then build your own.

Senra Jobs is a free, independent directory of the 50 guests interviewed on the David Senra podcast, the companies they founded, lead or are connected to, and the open roles those companies publish. It currently links 36,384 indexed roles and 84 companies with careers pages, and every application happens on the employer's own site.

David Senra studies founders, and about two-thirds of the founder guests researched for Senra Jobs were employees before they started their first documented business. Working for a great founder can be an apprenticeship: learn how a company is built from the inside, then go build your own.

Senra Jobs has no paid plans and no price for job seekers or employers. Browsing guests, companies and roles is free, and the site does not sell listings, placements or candidate data. The Find my role interview uses free daily credits only to limit abuse.

Role data on Senra Jobs comes from public employer job boards such as Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever and Workday, and the page shows when the boards were last fetched. The index is not a complete hiring inventory: some postings may have closed since the last fetch, and some employers have no supported public board.

In the Senra Jobs research, 27 of 41 founder guests (66%) worked for someone else before their earliest documented business, counting jobs, internships and apprenticeships. Research & sources ↗
