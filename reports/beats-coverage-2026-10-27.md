# Beats hiring coverage — October 27, 2026

The earlier acquisition-based exclusion was incorrect: Apple still advertises Beats team openings. The directory now links [Apple's Beats product category](https://jobs.apple.com/en-us/search?product=beats-audio-accessories-BTSAA) and indexes 17 distinct requisitions with explicit Beats duties.

## Collection and attribution

- Product category: 72 location postings over four pages; the final Next button was disabled.
- Keyword search: 30 location postings over two pages; the final Next button was disabled.
- Combined: 83 distinct location postings, 57 requisitions. All 83 public detail pages were retrieved.
- Included: 13 explicit Beats team roles and four roles whose descriptions explicitly include Beats alongside other Apple products. The latter display **Apple / Beats (shared product work)**.
- Not indexed: 40 requisitions whose broad category membership or keyword match did not establish a Beats assignment. They remain in the reconciliation evidence for further review; they are not silently treated as closed jobs.

Location variants were merged only after their titles and complete requirements matched. Published locations remain attached to the merged role. Ten roles have published base-pay ranges; differing Los Angeles and Bay Area ranges retain their exact location associations. The public wording does not explicitly state the pay period, so the data preserves the amounts without converting them into an annual or hourly comparison.

The [Automation Engineer posting](https://jobs.apple.com/en-us/details/200666875-2605/automation-engineer?team=HRDWR) was also checked in the browser: it identifies the Beats Engineering team, the Los Angeles location and five years of testing experience among its requirements. No application was started or submitted.

## Relationship and limits

[Apple's acquisition announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2014/05/28Apple-to-Acquire-Beats-Music-Beats-Electronics/) identifies Jimmy Iovine and Dr. Dre as co-founders. Current employer descriptions establish that Beats hiring continues inside Apple. This does not make every Apple job an Iovine-associated role.

Coverage remains partial because category-only assignments and other hiring channels are unresolved. This is a dated manual collection, with no automatic Apple adapter yet. The exact URL decisions, pagination captures and detail hashes are retained in the private working cache; public output contains only listing metadata and board receipts.
