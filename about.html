AboutAbout this directory.

Senra Jobs is a free, independent directory of the guests interviewed on the David Senra podcast, the companies they founded, lead or are connected to, and the open roles those companies publish. The idea is simple: working for a great founder can be an apprenticeship, so the directory makes it easy to find where those founders are hiring.

Senra Jobs is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by David Senra, Founders, or any company listed on the site. The episode index is a separate reading of the public Founders feed.

Who runs it

Adam Pang builds and operates Senra Jobs. His other work is at adampang.com, and the site's source code is public on GitHub.

Where the roles come from

Senra Jobs reads roles from employers' own public job boards and careers pages, such as Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever and Workday, and links each role to the employer's original posting. Senra Jobs does not write, edit or sell job listings, and it does not collect applications. The index is not a complete hiring inventory: some postings close between fetches, and some employers have no supported public board, so the directory links their careers page instead.

The research behind the apprenticeship idea, including sources for each guest, is on the research page.
